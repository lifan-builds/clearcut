About FreshPowder

The fastest way to spot deep ski and snowboard markdowns across independent mountain shops and major outfitters.

FreshPowder is part of Clearcut, built by Lifan Chen. Browsing is free, fast, and requires no account—equipment is front and center by default, with clothing and accessories one toggle away.

How we rank deals

We scan 28+ North American snow shops daily, match prices to the exact size in stock, and rank strictly by biggest percentage off first. Read more on how it works or check our buying guides.

Spot a missing shop or bad listing?

Open an issue on GitHub with the shop link or model you’d like added.

Want a heads-up when standout 60%+ markdowns drop? Join the launch list—see privacy & removal anytime.
